### T2.1 — 137 non-b₃-rooted formula review

**Scope.** The Arithma dependency walker reports 137 / 419 formulas don't
trace back to `b₃ = 24`. Per-formula classification into three buckets:

- **(a) Genuine algebraic identity** — no b₃ link needed (e.g. octonion
  associator = 0, Jacobi identity, modular S-matrix relations).
- **(b) Missing chain link** — formula does depend on b₃ but the
  `arithma_compact` field doesn't route through `b3_leaf()`. Fix is a
  codemod sweep that re-registers the formula with the correct chain.
- **(c) Hidden seed** — formula uses a numeric input that isn't currently
  in the Ten Pillar Seeds list but should be promoted (e.g. `dim(Leech) = 24`
  used as a literal rather than re-derived from b₃).

The 137 split roughly into three groups by subsystem:

#### T2.1.A — Group A (algebra / geometry, ~40 formulas)

**Subsystems touched:**
- `src/metaphysica/simulations/PM/algebra/octonions.py`
- `src/metaphysica/simulations/PM/algebra/e8_root_system.py`
- `src/metaphysica/simulations/PM/algebra/e8x8_splitting.py`
- `src/metaphysica/simulations/PM/algebra/leech_lattice.py`
- `src/metaphysica/simulations/PM/algebra/freudenthal_triple.py`

- `src/metaphysica/simulations/PM/geometry/modular_invariance.py`
- `src/metaphysica/simulations/PM/geometry/sphere_packing.py`

**Suggested approach.**
1. Dump the 40 formulas from this subset via:
   `python -c "import json; d=json.load(open('AutoGenerated/dependency_chains.json')); print([f for f in d['non_rooted'] if f['module'].startswith('algebra.') or f['module'].startswith('geometry.')])"`
2. For each, classify into (a/b/c) — most octonion / e8 / leech identities
   are genuine algebraic identities and should be tagged
   `arithma_rootless = "algebraic_identity"`.
3. Modular forms (`modular_invariance.py`) — many SL(2,Z) identities are
   algebraic. The few that compute `j(τ)` at special points may need the

**Files to touch (write):**

**Success criterion.**
- Of the ~40 formulas in this group, ≥ 90% are classified into (a/b/c).
- After re-registration, the b₃-rooted count rises by at least 25 (from
  277 → ≥ 302).
- A new test `tests/test_arithma_rootless_classifications.py` passes,
  enforcing that no algebra/geometry formula is left untagged.

   depends primarily on η.

   the inflation module's earlier path.

**Files to touch:**

  module; replace `derive_observables` with higher-order version)

**Success criterion.**

2. The non-perturbative W contributions are calculable from the b₃ Betti

3. Target m_{3/2} > 10 TeV to evade cosmological gravitino constraints
   and place soft scalar masses at TeV (LHC-accessible).

**Files to create.**

**Mechanism (a): Mirror-sector kinetic mixing via 27D bulk.**

- The S^{2,0} sampler data fields could carry an EDE component that
  contributes ~10% to the universe energy budget around z ~ 3000.
